described in README.md:

1. `python3 stress.py` deadlocks (deterministically — it forces the bad
   interleaving through the debug_hook instrumentation). Fix
   AccountBank.transfer so opposite concurrent transfers cannot deadlock.
2. AccountBank.audit must be deadlock-free against concurrent transfers
   AND must never return a torn snapshot (the snapshot's total must
   always equal the true total, even while a transfer is between its
   debit and credit).

Fix bankcore/accounts.py. Hard constraints (see README.md):
- Public API frozen; keep real locking (the grader hammers the bank from
  8 threads and compares the final state against a serial replay while
  an auditor thread continuously checks snapshot consistency).
- Keep the debug_hook attribute and BOTH call points exactly as
  documented ("transfer-first-lock" after the first lock acquisition,
  "transfer-mid" between debit and credit) — the grader drives them.
- stress.py and README.md are sha256-guarded; do not modify them.

When done, `python3 stress.py` must print OK. The grader additionally
forces an audit to run against a transfer paused at "transfer-mid" and
