Implement sched.py (workspace root, Python 3 stdlib only): a recurrence
scheduler for the FICTIONAL time zones defined in ZONES.json, following
RULES.md exactly. Do not use zoneinfo/pytz — these zones do not exist in
any tzdata; everything you need is in the two files.

The hard 20% is civil-time correctness:
- DST start rules are written in standard wall time, end rules in DST
  wall time.
- Local times inside a spring-forward gap map to the transition instant.
- Ambiguous fall-back times map to the EARLIER UTC instant.
- Zones include a +05:45 base offset and a 30-MINUTE DST delta.
- "day 31" monthly rules skip short months; "last fri" works in
  February; occurrences are STRICTLY after the start instant.

RULES.md contains four worked examples with exact expected output —
make all of them pass, then think hard about the transition weeks in
each zone. The grader runs your CLI on a few dozen hidden cases (many
sit on transition days) and compares output byte-for-byte.

ZONES.json and RULES.md are sha256-guarded READ ONLY.
