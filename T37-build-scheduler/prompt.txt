Implement scheduler.py (workspace root, Python 3 stdlib only): a
resource-constrained DAG scheduler per SPEC.md.

jobs.json in this workspace has 120 jobs in a layered dependency DAG
with cpu/mem requirements and a makespan_budget. Your schedule must
satisfy dependencies and both resource caps at every tick, and finish
within the budget. Serializing everything blows the budget several
times over; a real scheduling algorithm (SPEC.md sketches critical-path
list scheduling) is required.

Validate your own output before finishing: recompute resource usage
over time from your schedule and check every constraint — the grader
uses an independent validator, runs your scheduler on jobs.json AND on
hidden job sets from the same generator, and fails on the first
violated constraint or busted budget.

SPEC.md and jobs.json are sha256-guarded READ ONLY.
