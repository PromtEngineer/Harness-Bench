Deduplicate the identity records in people.tsv per the canonicalization
spec in SPEC.md, and write dedup.json in the workspace root.

Both files are sha256-guarded READ ONLY inputs; produce dedup.json with
a script you write in the workspace (Python 3 stdlib only — the
unicodedata module is your friend).

Follow SPEC.md to the letter. The data is a minefield of lookalikes:
composed vs decomposed accents, fullwidth letters, zero-width
characters, exotic spaces, ß/ss case folding, provider-specific email
rules — plus distractor pairs that LOOK similar but must NOT merge
(e.g. Turkish dotless ı, plus-suffixes on domains without the provider
rule). Getting "most of it" right still produces the wrong clusters:
the grader compares your dedup.json exactly (clusters and stats).

Sanity anchors to check yourself against: casefold not lower, NFKC not
NFC/NFD, and category-based (not codepoint-list) removal of format and
space characters.
